# Little Trail 🥾🌿
### Your little hiking buddy in Hong Kong.
### 你的香港徒步小夥伴。

Picking a trail is only the beginning. How do you get there? What should you pack? How much time and money will you need?

Little Trail brings these preparations together in a bilingual hiking planner, helping you spend less time sorting through information and more time looking forward to your next walk.

選好路線，只是出發的第一步。怎樣前往起點？需要帶甚麼？時間和預算是否足夠？

Little Trail 把零散的準備工作整理成一份中英雙語徒步計劃，讓你少花一點時間翻找資料，多留一點心情期待出發。

## 🌱 Made from a beginner’s experience
## 從初學者的經驗出發

I fell in love with hiking after moving to Hong Kong. As a beginner, I often spent more energy researching routes, transport, equipment and budgets than I expected. Little Trail grew from a simple wish: to make preparing for a hike feel more manageable.

來到香港後，我才愛上徒步。作為初學者，查找路線、交通、裝備和預算，往往比想像中更花心力。Little Trail 的起點很簡單：讓每次徒步前的準備，都更容易一些。

## 🗺️ Plan around you
## 按你的需要規劃

- **Your route, or a fresh suggestion**  
  Enter a preferred trail, or leave it open for recommendations.  
  填寫心儀路線，或留空讓規劃器提供建議。

- **Your time and experience**  
  Include your available hours, previous hike and how demanding it felt.  
  根據可用時間、上次徒步經驗及吃力程度，整理規劃需求。

- **The practical details**  
  Bring transport, equipment, food and budget considerations into one plan.  
  把交通、裝備、飲食與預算準備整合在同一份計劃中。

- **Optional shopping help**  
  Find retrieved outdoor-store and product links without turning your hike into a shopping itinerary.  
  提供查詢所得的戶外店與商品連結，不強制安排購物行程。

- **English + Traditional Chinese**  
  Read each part in English, followed by its Traditional Chinese translation.  
  英文下方附上繁體中文，方便對照閱讀。

## 📱 A simpler way to get ready
## 用手機，輕鬆準備

Complete three short sections:

填寫三個簡單部分：

1. **Trip details｜行程資料**
2. **Hiking experience｜徒步經驗**
3. **Budget & equipment｜預算與裝備**

The interface uses soft colours and clear labels, with a date picker and selectable effort ratings. Generated plans can be copied, downloaded as Markdown or printed to PDF.

介面採用柔和配色與清晰標籤，配備日期選擇器及徒步負荷選項。生成的計劃可複製、下載為 Markdown，或列印成 PDF。

## ⚙️ Behind the plan
## 計劃如何生成

The planning workflow is built with **Dify**, using **DeepSeek**, **Tavily** and **Hong Kong Observatory open data** to analyse requirements, retrieve information and assemble a bilingual plan.

規劃工作流以 **Dify** 建立，結合 **DeepSeek**、**Tavily** 及**香港天文台開放數據**，分析需求、查詢資料並整理雙語計劃。

This repository contains the custom front end and a server-side proxy template. API keys belong in the backend, never in public website files.

本倉庫包含自訂前端及後端中轉模板。API 金鑰須保存在後端，不應放入公開網頁檔案。

## 🌦️ Before you head out
## 出發之前

Little Trail supports preparation and decision-making. Check changing weather, trail notices, transport services and store availability before leaving. Information that cannot be confirmed should remain clearly marked as unverified. Use a dedicated map tool for navigation.

Little Trail 為準備與決策提供參考。出發前，請重新確認天氣、山徑通告、交通服務及門店存貨；無法確認的資訊應清楚標示為「未核實」。實際導航請使用專門的地圖工具。
